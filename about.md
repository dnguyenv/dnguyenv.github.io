I’m an engineer who enjoys building systems that scale, not just in performance, but in understanding. My work often lives at the intersection of infrastructure, intelligence, and abstraction. I care deeply about how ideas are represented, how knowledge is stored and retrieved, and how tools can amplify human thinking.
This blog is a space where I explore those themes through the lens of architecture, platform engineering, memory systems, graph-based knowledge, and the occasional philosophical tangent. It’s not a polished archive, it’s a working notebook, a place to think out loud and connect dots.

Whether you're here to dive into technical ideas or just curious about how systems shape thought, I’m glad you stopped by.
